Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Spiro ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit bespiro.com (the "Site"). It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information and remember your preferences. Cookies set by the site owner are called "first-party cookies"; cookies set by parties other than the site owner are called "third-party cookies".

How we use cookies

We keep cookies to a minimum. We use them to understand how the Site is used so we can improve it, and to remember basic preferences such as your chosen language. We do not use cookies to sell your personal data.

Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

These cookies are essential for the Site to function. They include lightweight storage we use to remember your language preference. They do not store any personally identifiable information.

How to manage cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you refuse or remove cookies; check your browser's help pages for instructions. Please note that disabling some cookies may affect how the Site works.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics across websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You can review or change your choice at any time: open cookie settings.
